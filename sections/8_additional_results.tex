\section{Experimental Protocols and Additional Results}
\label{sec:appendix-experiments}

This part records only empirical material that directly supports the main-text claims. It first fixes the completed CIFAR-100/ResNet18 protocol and CARAT hyperparameters, then reports final-loss results, a runtime diagnostic, and reproducibility details.

\subsection{Completed Empirical Scope}

The completed empirical scope is intentionally narrow. We evaluate untargeted model poisoning on CIFAR-100/ResNet18 with Dirichlet non-i.i.d.\ client partitions, $20\%$ malicious clients, and four attacks: ALIE, FangAttack, MinMax, and MinSum. The comparison uses Mean, NormClipping, MultiKrum, FLTrust, FLDetector, and CARAT. All claims are based on attacked final test accuracy, attacked final test loss, and average rank. CARAT and FLTrust use matched labeled server-reference budgets, unpublished or under-review defenses are excluded from the claim set, and the result-source policy is disclosed for each table rather than hidden in aggregate statistics.

\subsection{Reported CIFAR-100 Protocol}

Unless stated otherwise, the CIFAR-100 comparison uses $20$ clients with full participation, $200$ communication rounds, $4$ local epochs, learning rate $0.05$, and Dirichlet non-i.i.d.\ partitions with $\alpha \in \{1,0.5\}$. Legacy public-baseline rows use five random seeds where available, while regenerated FLTrust and CARAT rows use three random seeds under the matched reference-assisted protocol.

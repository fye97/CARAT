\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

CARAT validates candidate updates through hidden, class-balanced task certificates rather than relying only on geometric centrality or a single trusted reference direction. In the five-seed CIFAR-10/VGG19 and CIFAR-100/ResNet18 comparison with $10\%$ malicious clients, CARAT ranks first in $15$ of the $30$ attacked settings and second in the remaining $15$, with no seed at chance level. Its advantage grows as attacks become stronger: from $10\%$ to $40\%$ malicious clients it is the most accurate defense in $22$ of $24$ settings, and at $40\%$ it exceeds the strongest baseline by $7.6$--$10.1$ points on CIFAR-100 and on CIFAR-10 with $\alpha\ge0.5$. It leads every baseline at $40$ and $60$ clients on CIFAR-100, and with a held-out, budget-matched reference pool it stays within $0.25$ points of its original accuracy while exceeding FLTrust by $17$--$22$ points. The most heterogeneous CIFAR-10 setting at $30$--$40\%$ malicious clients remains challenging. Prior misspecification, certificate-aware adaptive attacks, and direct evidence for class-local filtering are natural next steps.

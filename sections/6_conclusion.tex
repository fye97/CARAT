\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

CARAT validates candidate updates through hidden, class-balanced task certificates rather than relying only on geometric centrality or a single trusted reference direction. In the five-seed CIFAR-100/ResNet18 evaluation, CARAT is competitive under the four conventional attacks for $\alpha\in\{1,0.5\}$ and ranks second among defenses with Mimic results. The expanded $\alpha=0.1$ evaluation reveals a different regime: CARAT remains strong under ALIE and MinMax but becomes highly seed-sensitive under FangAttack and MinSum. Together with the strong unprotected Mean results on the conventional attacks and the small component-ablation gaps, this evidence supports CARAT only as a conditionally effective reference-assisted mechanism. Disjoint reference data, matched auxiliary-data budgets, adaptive attacks, and methods that remain stable under extreme heterogeneity are important next steps.
